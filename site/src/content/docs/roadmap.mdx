Nothing of sounding is built yet. Each step ends with something running
on real work before the next one depends on it. A step is done when a
check passes or an observation holds. The step names are the plan's, in
agentplot/blueprints' `design/capture/plan.md`. sounding needs nothing
of millpond's until millpond sends it questions (Q5), so its own steps
proceed beside millpond's.

## sounding's own steps

<Steps>

1. **Q1 · The statement.** sounding's statement is written from the
   capture design: the question and its sources, the place, the three
   exits, the stop check, the timebox, rounds, the operator's decision,
   and what sounding never does. A fresh model reconstructs sounding
   from it, and the questions it asks are ruled and folded back in.

   _Needs_ the capture design's decisions ruled (D2). _Done when_ the
   reconstruction's questions are ruled and folded in, Vale is clean,
   and the statement lands on blueprints' `main`.

2. **Q2 · The repository's rules.** This repository, in Rust on
   kit-standard's code profile, gains its founding decisions: one
   question, one session, one conclusion; plugins outside the core; a
   conclusion is never a signal. With them come its rules, its sluice
   gate, `cliff.toml` and its OpenSpec configuration.

   _Needs_ Q1. _Done when_ sluice protects `main` and the first change
   lands through it.

3. **Q3 · The core and the Claude Code plugin.** `sounding open`,
   `list`, `conclude`, `offer`, `ask`, `accept`, `go-on` and `drop`,
   with the worktree, `question.md`, `conclusion.md` and `rounds/`. An
   offer prints capture lines with flywheel-next's capture crate and
   pipes them to `millpond land` where millpond is installed. The plugin
   brings `/sound`, the start hook, the stop check, the timebox, the
   MCP tools and the guard on `sounding/*` branches.

   _Needs_ Q2. _Done when_ a question opened with `/sound` in one
   session ends on a conclusion in another while the first session's
   work goes on undisturbed. A session that tries to stop with no
   conclusion is sent back, an offer's lines pass the conformance pack,
   and a sluice submission from a sounding's branch is refused.

4. **Q4 · The herdr plugin.** The sidebar of open soundings with their
   rounds and states, a workspace for each, and keys to start, open,
   accept, send back and drop one and to open its conclusion in
   plannotator.

   _Needs_ Q3. _Done when_ the plugin installs from sounding's tag, and
   a sounding started, sent back once and accepted from herdr shows each
   state in the sidebar.

</Steps>

## Where it meets the others

<Steps>

1. **Q5 · millpond sends a question to a sounding.** millpond's `send`
   takes a sounding as its target. millpond opens the sounding with the
   item's signals and captures as its sources, keeps the accepted
   conclusion as the item's record, and makes an item of each piece of
   work the conclusion proposes.

   _Needs_ millpond's sends (P6) and Q3. _Done when_ an item asking
   whether to take a major release goes to a sounding and its accepted
   conclusion shows on the item. The bump the conclusion proposes
   arrives as a new item, and curation answers the release's next
   signal by naming the conclusion.

2. **C6 · crew calls sounding.** crew's planner and design agent open a
   sounding for an item that asks a question, show each conclusion on
   crew's rail, and turn the work an accepted one proposes into plan
   entries.

   _Needs_ crew's amended record (C1), crew's curator on millpond (C4)
   and Q3. _Done when_ one real question goes from an item through a
   sounding to accepted plan entries on crew's rail, with no
   `<kit>/elaborations/` worktree made.

</Steps>

Flywheel follows on the same parts: once it hosts (F4 and F5), each of
an intent's elaborations runs as a sounding, and its elaboration types
write the conclusion into the design book and claims.
