## What each document is

- **The capture design**, `design/capture/design.md`, covers every part
  that turns an outside event into landed work: headwater, eddy,
  millpond, sounding, sluice, crew, Flywheel and the first fleet that
  runs them. Its section "Questions: sounding" is sounding's.
  `plan.md` beside it orders the work, and its decision 11 settles what
  sounding is.
- **sounding's statement**, `design/sounding/requirements.md`, is
  written from that design: the question and its sources, the place,
  the three exits, the stop check, the timebox, rounds, the operator's
  decision, and what sounding never does. A fresh model reconstructs
  sounding from the statement alone, and the questions it asks are
  ruled and folded back in before the statement lands. It comes next,
  as plan step Q1.
- **The design book** explains the design chapter by chapter, with the
  statement as its appendix.

## What binds the code

The statement binds. This repository cites its clauses by number and
never restates them. The rules a change here keeps, and the decisions
behind them, follow the statement into `docs/architecture/` and
`docs/adr/`. The founding decisions are three: one question, one
session, one conclusion; plugins outside the core; and a conclusion is
never a signal.

The capture contract belongs to Flywheel's statement, and
flywheel-next's capture crate implements it. An offer prints its lines
with that crate, so a question about an offer's lines goes to Flywheel's
statement first.
