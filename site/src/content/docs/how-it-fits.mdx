sounding takes the work that ends in an answer. millpond's items, crew's
planner and your own sessions send it questions, and its conclusions go
back as the record that settles them. Each tool stands alone, with its
own Claude Code and herdr plugins.

## The family

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="millpond · the inbox">
    Sends an item that asks a question to a sounding, with the item's
    signals and captures as its sources. It keeps the accepted
    conclusion as the item's record, makes an item of each piece of
    work the conclusion proposes, and lands each offer as a capture.
  </Card>
  <Card title="headwater and eddy · what is noticed">
    headwater reports releases, and a key on a release that needs
    judgment opens a sounding. eddy lists meetings, and `/sound` on a
    decision a meeting left open takes its transcript as a source.
  </Card>
  <Card title="sluice · landing">
    Lands the work a conclusion proposes, once it reaches an item's
    worktree or crew's plan. sluice never lands from a sounding's
    branch: the plugin refuses that submission.
  </Card>
  <Card title="crew · teams and plans">
    crew's planner and design agent open a sounding for an item that
    asks a question, as crew calls millpond. crew shows each conclusion
    on its rail and turns the work an accepted one proposes into plan
    entries.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Boomdrive · the umbrella">
    Each of an intent's elaborations runs as a sounding. Finishing with
    deliverables is `conclude`, blocking on a question is `ask`, and an
    offer is `offer`.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Where questions come from

| Setup | Questions from | Conclusions decided on |
|---|---|---|
| On its own | your `/sound` | a card, or sounding's herdr plugin |
| With millpond | items millpond sends; a key on a headwater release that needs judgment; `/sound` on a decision a meeting left open, its transcript a source | the same, recorded against the item |
| With crew | crew's planner and design agent, calling `sounding open` as crew calls millpond | crew's rail |
| With Boomdrive | an intent's elaborations, each one a sounding | Boomdrive's elaboration types, which write the conclusion into the design book and claims |

Moving from one setup to the next changes who sends the question and
where you decide the conclusion. The question, the worktree, the three
exits and the rounds stay the same.

## What sounding never does

- **It never lands code.** Prototype code stays on `sounding/<name>`.
  The work you accept reaches `main` through an item's worktree or
  crew's plan, and through sluice.
- **It never writes the inbox except through millpond.** An offer goes
  to `millpond land` like any other capture.
- **It never turns a conclusion into a signal.** An accepted conclusion
  is the record that settles signals, and only offers come back as
  signals.
- **It never plans across items.** crew's planner orders work today,
  and Boomdrive's does later.

## Built once, used everywhere

sounding holds the one implementation of a question, its rounds and the
shape of a conclusion. It grows out of crew's elaborations (crew ADR
0019), and crew and Boomdrive both build on it: crew calls sounding as
it calls millpond, and each of Boomdrive's elaborations is a sounding.
