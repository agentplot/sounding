A **sounding** is one question, worked by one agent session in one
place, ending on one **conclusion**. The operator decides each
conclusion. sounding is a standalone tool, and it works the same on its
own, with millpond, with crew or under Boomdrive.

## The question

`sounding open` writes `question.md`: the question and its sources. A
question reaches sounding three ways:

- **From millpond**, as an item that asks a question: whether to take a
  major release, or how to settle what a meeting left open. Its sources
  are the item's signals, the captures they came from, and any earlier
  conclusion on the subject.
- **From any Claude Code session**, with `/sound <question>`. The
  sounding opens while that session goes on with its own work.
- **From a key** in another tool's herdr plugin, such as headwater's on
  a release that needs judgment.

## The place

The session works in a worktree of the repository the question is
about, on the branch `sounding/<name>`. It may write prototype code
there to test an answer, and that code never lands from the sounding's
branch: sounding's Claude Code plugin refuses a sluice submission from
any `sounding/*` branch. The work you accept reaches `main` the way any
work does, through an item's worktree or crew's plan, and through
sluice.

## Three exits

<Diagram name="exits" caption="One round of one session. It asks you a question and waits, offers millpond a finding beyond its question, is sent back by the stop check, and concludes when its timebox runs out." />

The session leaves by one of three commands:

| Exit | What follows |
|---|---|
| `sounding conclude` | Writes `conclusion.md`: the answer, its evidence, what it would change, the work it proposes, and what stays open. You decide it. |
| `sounding offer` | Hands a finding beyond the question to `millpond land` as a capture of its own. The finding is its own excerpt, of kind `ask`, asserted by the sounding, under the key `sounding/<name>/<n>`. |
| `sounding ask` | Puts a question only you can answer on a card. The session waits for your answer. |

An offer keeps the session on its question. Whatever else it notices
goes to the inbox, where curation gives it a move like any other
signal.

Two guards hold each round to its question:

- **The stop check** keeps the session from ending without a conclusion
  or a note saying where the next round would start.
- **The timebox** limits each round. When it runs out, the session
  concludes with what it knows.

## Deciding a conclusion

<Rounds caption="Round 1 goes back with a note and is kept as rounds/1.md. Round 2 is accepted: millpond keeps it as the item's record, and the work it proposes arrives as two new items." />

You decide each conclusion on a card, in sounding's herdr plugin, or on
crew's rail:

- **Accept** settles the question. millpond keeps the conclusion as the
  item's record, and each piece of work the conclusion proposes becomes
  an item of its own, or an entry in crew's plan.
- **Go on** starts the next round in the same worktree. The conclusion
  moves to `rounds/<n>.md`, and your notes join the next session's
  question.
- **Drop** records the reason and removes the worktree.

## A conclusion is never a signal

Curation never judges a moved signal again, and nobody asks you to
approve the same thing twice. So an accepted conclusion enters the inbox
as the record that settles signals: when a later signal arrives on the
same subject, curation answers it by naming the conclusion. Only offers
come back as signals.

## Where you work with it

- **The command line:** `sounding open`, `list`, `conclude`, `offer`,
  `ask`, `accept`, `go-on` and `drop`.
- **The Claude Code plugin** gives every session `/sound <question>`. A
  sounding's own session gets its question, its sources and its earlier
  rounds from a hook at its start, and the stop check at its end. The
  plugin serves `soundings`, `conclusion`, `accept`, `go_on` and `drop`
  over MCP, and refuses a sluice submission from a `sounding/*` branch.
- **The herdr plugin** lists the open soundings in the sidebar, each
  with its round and its state: working, waiting on you, or parked. Each
  sounding has a workspace, with the session's pane and its conclusion
  beside it.

A sounding runs one of two ways: unattended, ending on a card, or with
you, in a pane beside you as a conversation (`--with-me`).
